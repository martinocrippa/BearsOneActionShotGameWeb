The full licence texts ship inside each npm package under `node_modules/` after `npm install`, and next to the font files in `public/assets/fonts/`.

Development-only tools (Vite, playwright-core) are not part of the published site.

ORSI! is a fan-made homage to the ideas and structure of DOOM (episodes, keys, secrets, cheat codes); it uses none of its graphics, sounds or maps and is not affiliated with id Software. Brands are not shown in the game: the fire extinguisher is generic "FIZZ" cola.
